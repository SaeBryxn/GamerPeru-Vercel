## Scope

The owner also authorized an explicit late-payment exception: when funds were received but stock
was unavailable, an operator with both `orders:verify` and `orders:manage` records a completed
external full refund with a mandatory declaration and external reference. This is a financial
recording action, not a payment transfer or bank reconciliation. It uses the existing `Refund`
entity and records the payment verification, refund, statuses, outbox events and audits in one
transaction. Released reservations stay released; no stock is invented or reallocated. If the
stock later returns after the external refund, the financial fact must still be recordable.

The `PaymentApproved` event for this exception suppresses its usual "pedido en preparación"
email. `RefundIssued` has no email template yet, so staff must notify the customer separately
through a secure channel. The admin form states this limitation.
