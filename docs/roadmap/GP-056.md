# GP-056 --- Mega-Menu Destinations

## Status

IMPLEMENTED --- 2026-10-16 ([closure](./GP-056-CLOSURE.md)); production verification pending
the push.

## Objective

Every link the storefront mega-menu renders resolves to a real, useful page: buying guides per
category, a software and drivers centre, a support hub and the official-store page. The static
navigation seed stops declaring brands, series and promos the catalog does not have, and the site
publishes a sitemap and robots policy.

## Why

The menu linked to ten routes that returned 404 (`/guia-de-compra/{mouses,teclados,mousepads}`,
`/software`, `/software/{mouse,teclado,dongle,firmware}`, `/soporte`, `/soporte/tienda-oficial`).
The admin already listed them as "páginas sin construir" (`missingDestinations`). The seed also
named brands the store does not carry (including WLMOUSE, against `AGENTS.md`); they never render
because `getStorefrontNavigation()` replaces them with catalog brands, but the seed should not
declare them at all.

## Dependencies

GP-009 (Global Navigation), GP-013 (Filters & Sorting), GP-039 (SEO Content Architecture),
GP-047 (SEO Audit), GP-051 (Search Engine Launch). The existing support pages
(`/soporte/{contacto,preguntas-frecuentes,distribuidores}`), the catalog reader and the category
specification schemas.

## Scope

1. **Buying guides** `/guia-de-compra/[slug]` for `mouses`, `teclados`, `mousepads`, from typed
   content in `src/content/guides`. Each criterion explains why it matters; each option is a
   catalog filter (`peso`, `forma`, `polling`, `bateria` for mouses; `superficie`, `largo`,
   `grosor` for mousepads; `formato`, `tipo_switch` for keyboards) validated against the
   category's live specification schema and counted with the catalog reader. Options the schema
   does not support or that match 0 products are not rendered. Article, breadcrumb and FAQ
   structured data.
2. **Software centre** `/software` and `/software/[tipo]` (`mouse`, `teclado`, `dongle`,
   `firmware`) from `src/content/software/brand-software.ts` for ATK, MCHOSE, JINMS and X-raypad:
   official configurator or download page per brand and kind, a contact fallback where a brand
   has no public URL, 8K dongle pairing steps and firmware precautions. Gamer Perú hosts no
   installers.
3. **Support hub** `/soporte` (FAQ, contact and WhatsApp through `ContactDetails`, orders,
   distributors, software, guides, warranty summary) and `/soporte/tienda-oficial` (Gamer Perú as
   the direct official channel in Peru: original sealed units, quality control, secure checkout,
   official contact channels, how to avoid unverified resellers, factory-defect coverage vs.
   misuse exclusions). No address, hours or legal terms that `company.ts` does not define.
4. **Navigation cleanup**: static brands, series and promos removed from
   `navigation-data.ts`; `missingDestinations` emptied; a unit test asserts that every link in
   `NAVIGATION_CONFIG` resolves to a route in `src/app` and that the seed declares no brand pages.
5. **SEO**: `src/app/sitemap.ts` (static storefront pages, guides, software, support, published
   categories, brands and products) and `src/app/robots.ts` (disallow admin, account, checkout,
   cart and API paths).

## Out of Scope

- Hosting firmware or driver binaries.
- Admin editing of guides or software links (typed repository content for now).
- Warranty periods or legal terms not defined in the repository.
- Series and promo cards beyond what `getStorefrontNavigation()` already derives from the
  catalog.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The ten routes return 200 with real content; no mega-menu link resolves to 404.
- Guide option links open the canonical filtered category URL and show the live product count;
  options with no products are hidden.
- Software pages list only typed, official destinations; brands without one show the contact
  fallback.
- `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` are served; private paths are disallowed.
- `menu-entries.ts` lists no missing destinations.

## Testing / Validation

Unit tests for guide option resolution, software content integrity, navigation link coverage
and sitemap/robots; E2E (fixture mode) for the ten routes, structured data, Axe and mobile
overflow; `pnpm check`, `pnpm test:e2e`.

## SEO DoD

Unique title, description and canonical per page; Article/BreadcrumbList/FAQPage JSON-LD on the
guides; sitemap and robots.

## Security DoD

Static content and public catalog reads only; external links use `rel="noopener noreferrer"`;
no customer or internal data on these pages.

## Accessibility DoD

One `h1` per page, landmark `main`, descriptive link names, Axe clean, no horizontal overflow at
375 px.
