## Status

COMPLETED

## Objective

Implement inventory truth.

## Why

This GP is a roadmap gate that reduces ambiguity for dependent work and
protects the canonical architecture.

## Context

Read `AGENTS.md`, the Master Spec, Roadmap, relevant
specs/design/architecture docs, accepted ADRs and prerequisite closure
reports. Later GPs are context only.

## Scope

GP-021 builds upon the checkout-critical physical inventory foundation introduced by GP-018 (inventory_locations, inventory_items, inventory_reservations, inventory_ledger_entries) without duplicating schema models (ADR-009). GP-021 owns the broader Inventory Engine: operational stock administration, inventory receiving, manual adjustments, damaged/quarantine workflows, stock allocation upon payment confirmation (ALLOCATED), background reservation expiration and release workflows (RELEASED), multi-location operational policy, low-stock signaling, and reconciliation tooling.

## Out of Scope

Checkout-critical reservation insertion kernel (owned by GP-018); external warehouse ERP integration.
