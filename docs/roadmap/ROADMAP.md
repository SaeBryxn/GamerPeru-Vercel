# Gamer Perú --- Roadmap v1.2

## Canonical execution rule

Only the explicitly assigned GP authorizes work. Dependencies are gates.
Later GPs provide context only. Every GP must close with a Closure
Report before dependent work is authorized.

## Important v1.2 additions

- GP-025 --- AI Product Research & Import.
- GP-035 --- AI Customer Assistant.
- Shared provider-agnostic AI architecture with configurable
  providers, models and AI Profiles.
- Customer Assistant privacy boundary: no unrestricted database or EC
  OS access.
- Canonical roadmap now contains GP-000 through GP-052.
